FAQ

Are you an AME or affiliated with the FAA?

No. This is educational guidance based on firsthand experience and process literacy. I don’t diagnose, provide medical advice, or guarantee outcomes. You still work with your AME and follow FAA requirements.

Will this tell me exactly what to say or what to submit?

No — intentionally. Exact wording and “templates” can create false confidence and lead to mistakes. This guide teaches how the process works, where delays come from, and how to make smarter decisions before you submit anything permanent.

What if I already submitted and I’m stuck?

That’s what the Recovery Call is for. We focus on understanding what stage you’re in, what’s missing or unclear, and what realistic next steps look like.

What should I do first?

If you’re unsure, start with the Risk Assessment. If you want the full framework, start with the guide. If you still can’t tell whether to proceed or pause after reading, then 1:1 support can make sense.
